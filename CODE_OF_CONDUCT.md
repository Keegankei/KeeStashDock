# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

KeeStashDock welcomes constructive participation from people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully, focus criticism on the work, give others room to learn, and protect private or sensitive information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private information, repeated disruption, or retaliating against a good-faith report.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project chat, release coordination, and public interactions where someone is representing KeeStashDock.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through a non-public contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not place sensitive details in a public issue. Include relevant links, dates, and a concise description of the behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, request corrective action, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation. Responses should be proportionate, documented when practical, and designed to protect affected participants. A maintainer involved in a report should not be the sole decision-maker when another qualified reviewer is available.

Good-faith reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves be treated as misconduct.
